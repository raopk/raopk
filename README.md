Data scientist and learning analytics researcher working on **fairness in AI**: how algorithms treat different groups of people, and how to measure and fix it when they don't.

### What I'm working on

- **Fairness in clinical early-warning models.** Measuring whether deterioration models warn some patient groups later than others, in hours, across outcomes and hospitals. Built on MIMIC-IV and eICU with Python and DuckDB.
- **Learning analytics.** Applying machine learning and data mining to learning management system data, with a focus on algorithmic bias and equitable outcomes for students.

### Background

- PhD in Educational Technology, University of Florida. Holmes Scholar and 2022 Gleim Research Fellow at the UF College of Education. Member of the AICE Lab (Advanced and Inclusive Computing for Education).
- MSc Economics and MSc Data Science, South Dakota State University. BSc Economics and Statistics, University of Cape Coast, Ghana.
- Six-plus years in data analytics, data management and visualization engineering in the USA.
